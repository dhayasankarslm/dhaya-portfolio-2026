# Companion route

The mascot follows a deterministic path down the page and stays in a narrow reserved margin. Scroll position selects a smooth interpolated location between five waypoints; it never chooses random positions. Randomness is limited to occasional expressions and blinks, while pupil direction follows the pointer. The companion disappears in the contact scene and while an overlay is open.

The left gutter remains available beside the site’s content lane. On phones, captions are hidden and the companion scales down. Reduced-motion preference stops its walking and blinking animation. The route tests check bounds across phone, tablet and desktop dimensions, endpoint clamping and transition continuity.
